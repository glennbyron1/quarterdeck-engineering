# Limitations and non-claims

## What this work does not establish

- Production readiness or a supported commercial release.
- Federal authorization, DoD accreditation or compliance certification.
- FIPS validation of the complete system.
- Official PIV conformance or universal card interoperability.
- Independent assurance or independent operator reproduction.
- Production CA/HSM custody and recovery.
- Complete Windows edition, patch and hardware support matrices.
- Complete installer/update/removal lifecycle across customer environments.
- A completed V3 or V4 implementation.

## Environment boundary

Results came from an isolated, owner-operated lab. The environment used
disposable systems, synthetic identities and intentionally bounded trust
hierarchies. Evidence may show that an exact configuration worked or failed;
it cannot prove behavior for every customer environment.

## Public-source boundary

This showcase intentionally excludes:

- IP addresses, hostnames and network diagrams that identify the private lab;
- credentials, recovery material and operational secrets;
- certificate serials and raw PKI evidence;
- private implementation repositories and installation packages;
- personal, employment or customer information; and
- details that would materially reduce the security of the lab.

## Project status

- V1: complete and frozen historical implementation reference.
- V2: complete and frozen author-produced lab milestone.
- V3: preserved but incomplete.
- V4: preserved concept and bounded experiment; paused at an authorization
  boundary finding.

This wording is intentional. The portfolio value comes from the engineering
decisions and evidence discipline—not from overstating completion.
